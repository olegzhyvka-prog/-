What "usable" means

Define the bar before rendering, in one sentence, and hold every tool to it. Without a fixed bar, the winner is whoever you liked.

What was held constant

Prompt, product, aspect ratio, resolution, and date. List them so the test can be repeated against you.

How cost was counted

Cost of every render, including the discarded ones, divided by clips that cleared the bar. Not the sticker price per generation.
